Modifications made (Apache-2.0 section 4(b))
--------------------------------------------
  * PP-OCRv5_mobile_det.onnx — converted from the official PaddlePaddle
    inference model (inference.json + inference.pdiparams) to ONNX with
    paddle2onnx, opset 14. No weights were retrained or altered.
  * PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec.onnx — converted from the official PaddlePaddle
    inference model to ONNX with paddle2onnx, opset 14. No weights were
    retrained or altered.
  * ppocrv5_dict.txt — the recognizer's character dictionary, extracted
    verbatim (one token per line) from the official PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec
    CTCLabelDecode configuration. 18383 entries.
